How to use this page.

Get your scoresheet

  1. Pick your Division and the Week. The page opens on the coming week.
  2. Pick My team to put your match at the top, marked "Your match". This browser remembers your division and team for next time.
  3. Tap Download (Front + Back). You get one PDF with both sides of the scoresheet; print both pages (double-sided if you can).

Each match shows the home team first and where it's played. A team on its bye week has no scoresheet; the page says "<team> has a BYE week"

The address bar keeps your division, week and team, so you can share the link or save it as a bookmark.

Files not showing up?

Refresh the page, or re-select your division and team. A week's scoresheets appear once they've been posted; if a week still says "No scoresheets", check back later.

Get them by email

Enter your email address and division under Get the scoresheets by email, complete the "I'm not a robot" check and tap Sign up. You'll get an email with a link: open it and tap Confirm. Sign-ups are reviewed before anyone is added to the mailing list.
